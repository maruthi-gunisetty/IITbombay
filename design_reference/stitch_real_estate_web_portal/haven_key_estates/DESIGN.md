## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, editorial minimalism tailored for high-net-worth property acquisitions, private listings, and premier residential advisory. The visual ethos merges the quiet authority of institutional brokerage with the tactile elegance of a modern luxury shelter magazine.

### Visual Principles
- **Architectural Symmetry:** Layouts prioritize disciplined structural framing, crisp divisions, and deliberate proportion over arbitrary ornamentation.
- **Editorial Poise:** Ample negative space allows high-resolution photography to serve as the primary emotional anchor, framed by rigorous typographic discipline.
- **Subdued Opulence:** Warm metallic accents are applied with restraint—acting strictly as focal beacons for status indicators, verification badges, and primary action triggers.
- **Uncompromised Clarity:** High contrast ratios, pristine hairline borders, and strict spatial rhythms evoke absolute transparency and operational trust.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep slate tones against luminescent champagne golds and warm, breathable neutrals.

### Roles and Application
- **Primary (`#0F172A` - Slate Navy):** The structural core. Used for dominant headings, structural framing, high-impact buttons, navigation bars, and key data points.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Burnished Champagne):** The primary metallic accent. Reserved for conversion anchors, active state outlines, exclusive tier callouts, and primary interaction indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Amber Gold):** Used sparingly for micro-highlights, hover illumination, verification badges, and star ratings.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Muted):** Governs secondary metadata, spatial borders, structural dividers, caption typography, and input borders.
- **Canvas & Surface Grounds:** The core background is set to `#F8FAFC`, stepping into `#F1F5F9` for secondary card backings, inset panels, and table rows. Primary card foregrounds remain pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to ensure maximum photographic pop and elevation contrast.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial gravitas of Newsreader with the crystalline, contemporary precision of Manrope.

### Typographic Roles
- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts (Newsreader):** Used for primary estate titles, architectural storytelling, section introductions, and large quotes. It introduces warmth, legacy, and high-craft editorial appeal.
- **Interface & Operational Text (Manrope):** Chosen for its exceptional legibility across dense spec sheets, floorplan dimensions, legal summaries, and navigation chrome.
- **Label Caps:** Formatted in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.1em`). Used exclusively for architectural status tags, MLS record categories, and section markers (e.g., "PENTHOUSE SUITE", "PRICE ON APPLICATION").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt architectural rhythm, utilizing a responsive 12-column grid system designed to support asymmetric, magazine-style property spreads alongside utilitarian filter layouts.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 48px (`margin`) outer padding, 24px (`gutter`) column separation. Max canvas width clamped to 1440px for comfortable reading lines.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 32px outer padding, 20px gutters. Property details transition from side-by-side split panels to stacked master sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px (`margin-mobile`) outer padding, 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters. Sticky bottom CTA bars handle scheduling and valuation requests.

### Spacing Rhythm
- `space-xs` (4px): In-badge icon gaps, inline metric separator dots.
- `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-label spacing, compact stack grouping inside table cells.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard form field padding, listing tag cluster separation.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card interior padding, filter column gutters.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section segment breaks, photo-grid hero margins.

## Elevation & Depth

To convey architectural discipline, depth is established through crisp, hairline boundaries and micro-diffused ambient drop shadows rather than heavy blurs.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` foundation.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Navigation):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Hover & Flyouts):** `#FFFFFF` with border shifted to `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Curated Lightboxes, Inspection Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` with 1px border of `#0F172A` at 10% opacity. Shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a 40% opacity slate navy backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).

## Shapes

The geometric expression utilizes subtle, softened corners (Level 1: Soft) to preserve architectural structure without feeling industrial or sharp.

### Radius Metrics
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Pills):** `0.25rem` (4px) base curvature.
- **Cards & Data Tables:** `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px) for structural composure.
- **Modal Panels & Large Hero Insets:** `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- **Luxury Badges:** May override to fully circular or `rounded-full` exclusively for floating verification seals, broker avatar portraits, and bookmark toggles.

## Components

### Buttons & Conversion CTAs
- **Primary Action (Direct Acquisition / Tour Booking):** `#0F172A` slate navy fill, `#FFFFFF` text, 4px corner radius. Padding: 12px 24px. Hover state: fills to `#1E293B` with a subtle bottom shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Champagne Emphasis):** `#D97706` fill with white text, or pure `#0F172A` outline (1.5px) on a transparent canvas. Hover state shifts the border and text to `#D97706`.
- **Ghost Action (Brochure Downloads, Share):** Transparent background, 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, `#0F172A` text.

### Luxury Status Chips & Badges
- **Curated / Exclusive Badge:** `#FEF3C7` background with `#92400E` text and a subtle 1px border of `#FDE68A`. Typographic role: `label-caps`.
- **Architectural Specification Tag:** `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text, 4px border radius, 4px 8px padding. Used for: "4 BEDS", "5 BATHS", "6,200 SQ FT".

### Form Controls & Property Inquiries
- **Inputs:** Minimalist white containers with a 1px border in `#CBD5E1`. Height: 48px. Text is `body-md` in `#0F172A`. Active focus transitions the border to `#0F172A` with a 1px ring in `#D97706` at 20% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hairline geometric borders. Active state fills with `#0F172A`, displaying a crisp white micro-check.

### Property Showcase Cards
- White `#FFFFFF` ground enclosed by a 1px `#E2E8F0` hairline border.
- Image aspect ratio locked strictly to 16:10 or 4:3 with inset heart icon (floating frosted glass badge: `#FFFFFF` at 85% opacity with 4px backdrop blur).
- Content block: `label-caps` for neighborhood, `headline-sm` for pricing, and `body-sm` for bed/bath/sqft metadata separated by muted vertical rules (`#E2E8F0`).

### Additional Estate Components
- **Floorplan Explorer:** Split-view layout featuring scalable SVG blueprints on the left and room-specific finish galleries on the right.
- **Agent Authority Tile:** Compact inset card featuring a monochrome portrait, institutional license credentials, closed volume statistics, and instant private message hooks.
